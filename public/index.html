Start here

Into the Batcave

  1. Sign in at the Batcave, Newsletter tab.
  2. Your drafts, then Restore from backup.
  3. Pick batcave-restore-content-kit.json.
  4. The issue appears in the issue list. The 11 posts appear on the Blog posts tab.

The ids are new, so nothing you already have is overwritten.

Posts into Shopify

  1. Content, Blog posts, Add blog post. Pick the blog The Batcave and the theme template bat-city.
  2. Press the <> button and paste the body from below (or the file in posts/).
  3. Fill the other fields from the rows. Leave it a draft.

Pages into Shopify

  1. Content, Pages. Open the page (or add it).
  2. Press <> and paste the content. Subheadings are already Heading 2.
  3. Set the search fields from the rows.

The newsletter

  1. Edit it in the composer (Newsletter tab) and use Copy for Shopify.
  2. Or paste newsletter/nov5-final-copy-DRAFT-email.html into Shopify Messaging, Code your own. It still has prompts in square brackets.

What changed on the way in

  • Words are verbatim. Typos, brackets such as [FILL: ...] and [LINK: ...], and stale dates came across as written. The composer's checks list will show them.
  • Tables became lists in the guides. The post composer has no table block. Each table row is now a bold lead-in plus a sentence. The pages keep real tables.
  • Small cleaning. Bare ampersands became &amp; and double spaces collapsed, because that is what the composer does to any body.
  • Guides have no excerpt, picture description or search fields in their Docs, so those are empty. Handles are empty too, so Shopify builds them from the title.
  • Family Game Night is about 2,000 words against the guide aim of 1,000. The Darkseid capsule is 79 words, under the 100 a capsule needs.
